The loaded PluginManager list and cards consume three canonical recipes generated
from the actual published DSH 0.2.1-rc.2 package at reference commit
687f282e8625c6b8d11ff58211a90524be2e4317. The validated implementation is

The implementation preserves natural title/tag wrapping, status tags, independent
open and switch actions, source contour hit bounds, artwork fallback and keyboard
focus visibility. Descriptions wrap the original text before adding an ellipsis;
the retained browser controls also cover collapsed whitespace, clipped unbroken
words, accented text and CJK. Measurement-only word joiners never enter the painted
or accessible text. Broader Unicode, bidi and platform text behavior remain open.

`receipt.json` contains the separately computed fixed 99.5% results, raw RGBA
differences, source inventory, remaining debt and preservation counts. The complete

`cards/`. A passing vendor status or equal viewport dimensions is not a substitute
for the fixed target or a component geometry comparison. Every pending frame is
retained; no fonts, colors, classifier, thresholds or capture fit were adjusted.

The final 224 pairs have equal dimensions; 166 reach the fixed non-text 99.5%
target. Their non-text mean is 99.59141071428571% and RGB mean is
99.82754910714286%. The old 108/96 cohorts retain all 408 provider images with
identical RGBA. Real Manager and Tag/Switch before/after changes are reported
separately rather than assumed unchanged.

All thirteen final checks and four normal/fixture builds passed. Hardware GPU
replay passed 112 assertions and retained 80 PNGs. Android passed 17 scenes,
57 checks and 30 literal inputs. The normal APK was restored and its installed
